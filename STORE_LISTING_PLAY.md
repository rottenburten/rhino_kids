## EN (English)

### App name (max 30)
Rhino Kids

### Short description (max 80)
Preschool math adventure: counting, addition, shapes and more

### Full description (max 4000)
Rhino Kids is a gentle, ad-free math adventure made for preschool and kindergarten children. Set in a warm savanna world, it turns early math into play, so young learners build real number skills while having fun with Reno the rhino and friends.

Designed for ages 3 to 6, Rhino Kids introduces core early-math concepts step by step, with friendly animal characters and animated visuals that bring every operation to life.

LEARNING MODULES
Eight playful modules cover the foundations of preschool and early elementary math:
- Counting with Zara the Giraffe: number recognition and counting
- Addition with Eko the Elephant: first sums
- Subtraction with Zigi the Zebra: taking away
- Multiplication with Leo the Lion: times tables made simple
- Division with Momo the Monkey: sharing equally
- Sequences with Kira the Eagle: find the missing number
- Shapes with Tomi the Turtle: recognize shapes
- Telling time with Kalo the Kangaroo: read the clock

THREE DIFFICULTY LEVELS
Easy, Medium and Hard let the game grow with your child, from first numbers to more confident problem solving.

ANIMATED, VISUAL TEACHING
Instead of dry drills, Rhino Kids shows math visually. Animated characters and clear illustrations help children understand what counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication and division really mean.

REWARDS THAT MOTIVATE
Stars, streaks and a badge collection keep children encouraged and proud of their progress, without pressure.

PARENT PANEL
Behind a simple parent gate, grown-ups can:
- Track progress with weekly reports and per-module statistics
- Set a daily play-time limit to manage screen time
- Choose the language and personalize your child's name
- Reset data at any time

SAFE AND PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Rhino Kids is built to be child-safe:
- No ads and no third-party advertising
- No data collection: everything stays on the device
- Works fully offline
- A parent gate protects settings and purchases

LANGUAGES
Available in English, Turkish and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.

SUBSCRIPTION (OPTIONAL)
Rhino Kids offers an optional subscription that unlocks all modules and features. A 3-day free trial is available; cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges. Manage or cancel anytime in Google Play, under Account and then Subscriptions.

Whether your child is just starting to count or practicing addition and times tables, Rhino Kids makes early math a happy, screen-time-friendly adventure in the savanna.
